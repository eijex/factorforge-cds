                        Scientific Evidence & Experimental Boundaries
                    
            
                    
              
                        
            • In-Silico Artifacts Only: Wet-lab validation is mandatory before experimental use.
              • 
                        
            • No Kinetic Guarantees: Metrics, 4DP (+35 aa), and pLDDT do not establish expression, folding, or yield.
              • 
                        
            • Regulatory & IP: Users are responsible for FTO, biosecurity screening, and compliance.
              • 
                    
            
                
            

                
                
            
                    
            
                        
                        Data Privacy & Confidentiality
                    
            
                    
              
                        
            • Ephemeral Processing: Inputs are processed in-memory and saved only in local browser history; do not submit confidential sequences.
              • 
                        
            • Non-Confidential Feedback: Wet-lab / GitHub submissions must remain coarse and non-confidential.
