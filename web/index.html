                        Scientific Evidence & Experimental Boundaries
                    

                    
    
                        
  • In-Silico Review Artifacts Only: FactorForge produces computational CDS candidates and pre-synthesis review artifacts. Independent scientific review and empirical wet-lab validation are mandatory prior to relying on any design in experiments.
    • 
                        
  • No Biological or Kinetic Guarantees: In-silico metrics, codon adaptation profiles, 4DP structural pause intervals (+35 aa modeling assumption), and AlphaFold pLDDT scores do not establish in vivo translation rates, folding kinetics, protein yield, glycosylation, regulatory clearance, or biological activity.
    • 
                        
  • Regulatory & IP Responsibility: Users are solely responsible for verifying intellectual property freedom-to-operate, biosecurity screening, and regulatory compliance for their sequences.
    • 
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        
                        Data Privacy & Confidentiality Protection
                    

                    
    
                        
  • Processing & Browser History: Submitted inputs are sent to the API for processing. Generated sequences and review results may be saved in this browser's local history until you clear it. This notice does not certify infrastructure-wide zero retention. Do not submit confidential sequences through this public interface.
    • 
                        
  • Strict Non-Confidential Feedback: Public wet-lab feedback and GitHub issue submissions must be coarse and non-confidential. Do not submit raw sequences, construct IDs, target identities, exact process parameters, patient data, or partner/customer confidential information.
    • 
                    

                

            


            
            

                

                    

                        
                        Design Modes, Compute & Research Collaboration
                    

                    
                        Request Collaboration (eijex.lab@gmail.com)
                    
                

                

                    1DP is the standard sequence-only CPU design path. 3DP / 4DP are experimental research implementations; general web access is on hold pending evaluation and collaborator feedback. Local research code may be included in the package; its presence is not a supported public web feature. Optimization from prepared structural inputs or reviewed boundaries does not inherently require GCP or a GPU.
                

                

                    Compute boundary: CPU optimization from prepared inputs is separate from automatic structure prediction, which may require a GPU service. Hosting the web interface on Vercel does not enable every research engine: web availability depends on deployed API support and execution limits. These implementations do not establish calibrated translation dynamics or demonstrated folding or expression benefits.
                

                

                    Lab-specific design criteria: FactorForge applies its own default design and review criteria, not an approved SOP for any named laboratory. To discuss your laboratory's SOP, candidate sequence generation or potential 3DP/4DP research collaboration, send a non-confidential enquiry to eijex.lab@gmail.com. Feasibility, scope and data handling must be agreed before work begins; do not include confidential or proprietary sequences in the initial email.
                

                

                    
Sequence Rights & Data Use: Rights and permitted use of submitted sequences and generated candidates, access, retention and deletion terms must be agreed separately before data is shared. Research, model training, publication or public disclosure requires prior explicit consent covering the purpose and disclosure scope. An enquiry or generation request is not consent to those uses or a transfer of rights. The software license does not automatically transfer ownership of sequence outputs to Eijex.
