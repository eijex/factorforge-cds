DP v2.1.1 retains the three-dimensional state `(i, h, q)`: codon position,
cumulative GC count, and Aho-Corasick automaton state. At codon 15 it prunes
the active layer to the requested 45-nt GC count band (9-13 bases by default),
clamped when the protein's synonymous envelope or automaton reachability makes
that band unavailable. The result reports whether clamping or relaxation was
required. Proteins shorter than 15 codons do not reach the layer-15 guard.

The same automaton rejects `AAAAAA`, `TTTTTT`, `GGGGGG`, and `CCCCCC`, including
cross-codon occurrences. Configured Type IIS motifs remain hard vetoes. The
engine also reports 30/45-nt GC, 50-bp sliding-window GC, global-GC boundary
status, homopolymer length, and 5′ MFE availability/value from the independent
ViennaRNA evaluation stage.

Calibration on Target-mAb-A LC/HC preserved translation, removed >=6-nt
homopolymers, and produced zero configured BsaI/BsmBI/BpiI sites. These are
in-silico sequence and modeled-folding observations on the calibration target,
not evidence of ribosome scanning, expression, yield, synthesis acceptance, or
wet-lab performance. Generalization remains unverified until the frozen holdout
benchmark is completed.
